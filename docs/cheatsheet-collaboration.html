Questions and replies travel through the file bus and tmux courier. The first-team walkthrough explains the pane flashes while mail waits.

Choreography over orchestration: agents agree on who does what, work with their peers, and ask you when a decision needs you. TAP carries their messages; the agents choose the next move.

Give each agent a job

Leader

Sets the goal, picks the team, and settles decisions the team cannot.

Driver

Carries a piece of work from implementation through review and delivery.

Reviewer

Reads the work independently and reports concrete problems.

Partner

Thinks alongside another agent and challenges the approach as it develops.

Conductor

Tracks progress, routes reviews, and brings blockers to the leader.

You

Choose the goal and make the calls that need your judgment.
