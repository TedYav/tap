**`delivery` (optional; absent = `pane`).** The member's delivery class. `pane` is everything above: delivery = durable write + sentinel injection into the registered pane. `poll` is a pane-less member (a daemon — e.g. the hermes gateway): delivery = the durable payload write alone; **no pane, no injection, no sentinel**. The member discovers work by running `tap poll <handle>` on its own schedule (plus the optional wake hint, §Poll-mode delivery class).

**Crew working-directory membership (tap-mxf95).** Before creating a window,
`crew add` resolves the requested cwd through the shared caller selector without
an ambient `TAP_HOME` fallback. Its acting registry must match the launcher's
canonical acting registry. A different or unresolvable registry refuses with
`crew_registry_mismatch` and guidance to use a cwd in the launcher's team or
launch from the intended team. No pane or seat is created by this refusal;
`--override-worktree` does not override team membership. Existing cancellation
and settlement authority is unchanged.

**`crew` (optional; only literal `true` has meaning).** A crew member belongs to one teammate and lives no longer than it; restarting a crew member means adding it again and re-briefing it. `tap crew add <preset|harness> [name] [--model <m>] [--cwd <path>]` creates a window in the teammate's tmux session, pre-registers that pane, and starts the harness through the launcher's invocation table. That bootstrap registration stamps the explicitly requested harness as the row's expected family while retaining the shell as raw pre-exec provenance; point-of-effect authority remains blocked until the requested harness is positively observed. A live exact-handle collision rolls back the suffixed row and new window; a malformed receipt leaves the dead row for explicit refresh rather than risk deleting an incumbent. `tap register <role> --crew` marks the row directly. `--crew` is incompatible with poll delivery and offline mode. An existing active binding for the exact pane wins before session-name derivation, preserving a pre-registered crew role through idempotent SessionStart registration.

New crews carry a [durable owner link and native capture outcome](crew-ownership.md).
