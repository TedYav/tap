**Per-cycle pane authority.** At the start of every tick, watch reads the active-row candidates once and runs each through `tap_seat_observed_record`. Only observed `active` rows enter that tick's shared handle/harness cache. Every transcript-reading advisory, evaluator transcript walk, and ordinary roster-driven seat pass consumes that cache. A stale boot/server stamp, absent exact session, designated-pane disagreement, exited harness, or unobservable result therefore skips the seat for the cycle before `capture-pane`; the tick does not persist a lifecycle rewrite.

**Reserved identity + consent.** The supervisor's sender identity is the reserved token `watcher`: `tap register` refuses it as a seat handle (`error_class=reserved_handle`), so a pane-less supervisor can never be claimed as a registered seat and inherit a real seat's delivery semantics. The reason is the identity boundary, not whatever a plain send stamps by default. A non-`none` evaluator prints and records a pane-transcript-egress consent line at arm time.

**Probe acceptance state machine.** Watch calls `tap send --json` and branches on the exact structured result, never rc0 alone: `0/injected,pane`, `0/queued,harness-queue` with `turn=unconfirmed` (the payload is canonical; the turn stays unconfirmed and is never recorded as injected), `0/queued,poll` (a certified pre-effect `identity_mismatch`/`pane_gone` park whose canonical is already placed, so a retry would mint a second probe), and `5/pending,<empty delivery>` record the rung and start `probe_timeout`; `0/queued,spool` records one exact probe id with no response timer until canonical/settled state, and every watcher-probe park path (including visible pending input) receives a `deadline_epoch` exactly `probe_timeout_seconds` after first park. A live redelivery claim remains authoritative even after the payload moves canonical; only claim release/stale-claim reconciliation permits the watcher transition. If deadline redelivery still cannot land, the entry sets `escalated_at` and raises one sticky NOTIFY through spool/per-entry dedup, so an acknowledged earlier episode alert cannot consume it — and for the watcher's own queued probe this deadline disposition runs even when the recipient pane's readiness is one of the redelivery holds (`busy:queued`, `busy:recovery`, `busy:decision-unavailable`, `busy:operator-wait`): the probe's deadline mark is an alert obligation, not redeliverable mail, so it takes that disposition with no redelivery attempt while every other entry keeps the hold-until-redeliverable skip (tap-oir29). Exit 3 skips the vanished seat. Exit 1 consumes the rung and notifies only for intrinsic `unsafe_target` / `unsupported_harness`; repairable classes (`seat_pending`, `tmux_unreachable`, `identity_mismatch`, and other nonterminal rc1 results) leave the rung retryable and raise no sticky notice. There is no rc4 defer counter or two-strike ladder in v0.7. Notice/continue sends treat both exit-0 dispositions as durable without claiming a queued send was injected. The probe and the goalie's judge and verdict sends read the courier result through one exact-tuple parser (`courierDisposition`): `injected,pane`; `queued` via `spool`, `poll`, or `harness-queue` with `turn=unconfirmed`; and rc5 `pending` are durable, and a malformed or unknown result refuses.

**Disarm ownership (tap-a4i, replacing R18's permissive non-armer branch).** Self-disarm by the armer is silent. **The actor is the calling pane, never `--by` or `TAP_SELF` (tap-calo):** both are caller-supplied, so honoring them let a peer run `tap watch off --by <armer>` and disarm as the armer; `--by` is still parsed (old scripts do not hit `unknown_option`) but grants nothing on `off`, and the `already_armed` door offers `tap watch off --force`, never the armer's handle. A non-armer — including an unknown/unresolvable actor — gets `error_class=watch_off_refused`; the action names the armer and `--force`, and the lease stays intact. The one exception is an ownerless lease (legacy or malformed, no `armed_by`): it has no armer to protect or notify, so `offGuarded` disarms it silently without `--force` for any caller (oracle row `off-no-armed-by`). `--force` is the dead-armer escape: it restores the old disarm + `kind=notice` path (sticky NOTIFY fallback if delivery fails). Evaluator-internal disarm and reset/rearm lease machinery remain direct and unaffected.

**Sticky NOTIFY.** A human NOTIFY (chain exhaustion or another explicit supervision escalation) is **sticky until an explicit `tap watch ack` or an armer disarm** — nothing else clears it. An episode reset must not touch it, and a later NOTIFY raised while one is still unacked **merges** (the first `at`/`reason` and `acked=0` are preserved) rather than clobbering the pending escalation: a discarded human escalation is exactly the degraded-safety-net-gone-silent failure R18 exists to prevent.

**Blocked-seat classification (tap-dst).** A stopped seat is not always merely idle — it can be positively *blocked* with a known (or unknown) remedy, and probing it is noise the ladder should skip. Detection reads pane transcripts via `tap peek`, same as the evaluator, under the same arm-time consent (§Transport/supervision boundary) — it never lives in transport/`lib.sh`. Right before a rung would be probed, its transcript is matched against an (pattern, reason) ladder — `session_limit` (a parseable "hit your session limit · resets `<H:MMam/pm>` (`<tz name>`)" clause) outranks the generic `api_error` ("terminated early due to an API error" with no recognized reset clause) — and a match is recorded into `blocked_map` instead of probed.

Blocking is **per-seat, not per-team**: a blocked seat is skipped by the rung-selection skip-set (merged with `probed_seats`) so OTHER seats keep escalating normally, and the chain-"exhaustion" human NOTIFY is likewise scoped **per-seat, not team-wide** (tap-38j): a seat that was genuinely probed-and-exhausted (in `probed_seats`, and NOT itself in `blocked_map`/`blocked_exempt`) NOTIFYs even while a DIFFERENT seat sits on a still-pending session-limit timer — the blocked seat has a known remedy at a known time and is not "exhausted", but the wedged one is. The NOTIFY is deferred only when the exhaustion is attributable **solely** to blocked seats (no probed-and-exhausted seat outside `blocked_map`/`blocked_exempt`); with `blocked_map` empty the ordinary "no eligible seats → human rung" exhaustion is unchanged. A per-seat timer pass (`_watch_process_blocked`) runs every tick, unconditionally, independent of episode/silence/**run-pause** state, and processes each blocked seat's own fire time. The spool redelivery pass has the same run-pause independence; `pause` gates neither maintenance pass. The [approval-timeout](../delivery/approval-timeout.md) pass runs right after the blocked-seat pass on every full tick, with the same independence, and costs one policy read for a team that has not opted in.

- **`session_limit`** parses the 12h-clock + tz-name-in-parens reset clause (the runtime's IANA database, the zone in its own spelling, rolling to the next occurrence if already past) into `reset_at`; an unparseable clause (unknown tz, unmatched time) is **demoted to `api_error`** rather than arming a `reset_at=0` timer that would fire — and re-fire — every threshold forever. Fire time is `reset_at + grace` (default 60s, `TAP_WATCH_BLOCKED_GRACE`).
- **`api_error`** has no parseable end time: fire time is `since + backoff` (default 120s, `TAP_WATCH_BLOCKED_BACKOFF`).
- At fire time, the seat's CURRENT state is checked first: if it no longer reads `idle-at-prompt`, or its pane's `window_activity` has advanced past `since`, it *looks* recovered. But `window_activity` is window-scoped — **incoming** traffic bumps it too — and a non-idle state can itself be the block banner, so the watcher **re-reads the pane before clearing** (tap-vd0): only when the block banner is genuinely **gone** is the entry cleared with **no send** (a clobbering continue would interrupt a seat that is already moving). If the banner **still classifies**, a stray teammate send faked the recovery — the seat is moved to `blocked_exempt` (never cleared-and-forgotten, which would let the next tick re-classify off the still-visible banner and roll its now-past `reset_at` +24h) and falls through to the ordinary probe/NOTIFY ladder. Otherwise (past the timer, not recovered) the watcher sends the seat one mechanical, self-contained, settle-only **continue** nudge (`watch-templates/continue.md`, riding `kind=probe` — a liveness poke to a possibly-stuck seat is exactly what a resume nudge is, so this reuses the existing reserved kind rather than minting a third one), removes the seat from `blocked_map`, and adds it to `blocked_exempt`. The continue body is settlement-first; routine status is prohibited, blocker/done exceptions route only to the armer, and the seat is never invited to disarm. The blocked-map/exempt transition is **persisted before** the continue send, so a tick killed mid-pass can never re-send a duplicate continue.
- `blocked_exempt` membership is checked by the classify-before-probe step: an exempt seat is **never reclassified**, even if the same banner that originally blocked it is still on screen — it falls straight through to an ordinary probe and the normal ladder/NOTIFY from there. This is the fix for a real bug: an earlier version cleared this memory via the general episode-reset helper, so a seat whose banner never scrolls away (a genuinely dead session) re-blocked itself off the same stale evidence on every subsequent wedge cycle, forever, and NOTIFY never fired. `_watch_process_blocked` never calls the episode-reset helper — that separation is the fix. `blocked_map`/`blocked_exempt` ARE cleared by `tap watch on`/`--rearm` and by a genuine (real bus/repo activity) episode reset elsewhere in `tick`, since that is positive evidence the team moved and every seat earns a fresh classification slate.
- A seat that just received a continue nudge is skip-set for rung-selection **for that one tick only** (not persisted) — otherwise it could receive an ordinary probe moments after the continue in the same tick, or a same-tick chain "exhaustion" could NOTIFY before the seat had any chance to respond. It becomes probe-eligible starting the next tick.

v1 ships `session_limit`/`api_error` off a live-captured Claude Code fixture; the per-harness banner table and the escape-abort taxonomy (steering vs. accidental-abort — see tap-dst) are shaped to slot into the same (pattern, reason) ladder but are not yet implemented pending captured evidence for their detection patterns.
