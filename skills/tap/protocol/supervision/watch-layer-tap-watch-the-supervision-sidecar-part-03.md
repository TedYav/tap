escalation can resume only after one fresh `threshold_seconds` window. `tap watch off` deletes the lease + best-effort kills the sidecar window, but is armer-owned unless a non-armer explicitly passes `--force`. `status` prints the arm/pause record plus a `dispatcher: alive|dead|unknown` line from the lease's `watcher_pid`, `watcher_command` and `heartbeat_at` (a `next:` recovery line follows `dead` and `unknown`); a `dead` dispatcher is reported state and exits 0 with its `next:` line (CLI-EXIT-0; `tap health` is the check verb that exits non-zero for it), while `unknown` — the lease or the process table could not be read — never reads as dead and exits 1 (unsafe to check, CLI-EXIT-1), and `alive` and disarmed exit 0; `ack` clears sticky NOTIFY. `tick` is one pure evaluation over (bus, repo, injected clock); `run` is `while lease: tick; sleep`. Concurrent `on`s serialize on an atomic-`mkdir` mutex and skip a second spawn. **Self-heal:** a re-`on` whose sidecar window exists but whose watcher process is dead or whose heartbeat is stale kills the corpse window and respawns the loop — the supervisor recovers its own death. Root-derived legacy-only buses refuse `old_store` during selection. Explicit custom `TAP_HOME` paths follow the ordinary bus contract; watch does not classify them by spelling.
