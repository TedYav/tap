**Changes since v0.11 (the v0.12 team-qualified receive gate — tap-18so):** a courier-stamped `to_team` now participates in recipient authority instead of serving as display metadata only. A foreign-team sender-bus canonical whose bare `to` happens to match a local handle is outbound relay state: `tap poll` and the dispatcher pending-age warning exclude it, while sender-side `tap outstanding` retains it with an `outbound-relay` label (`outbound_relay: true` in JSON). `tap read` and `tap reply` require the resolved recipient team and actor handle to match `to_team`/`to` before writing a receipt or sending an answer; a refusal names the addressed `<team>/<handle>` and the absolute payload-path recovery. Bare-id `tap reply` can resolve a unique enrolled-bus payload; an explicit payload path remains a recovery option. Payloads without `to_team` preserve legacy/manual-relay handle-only behavior.

**Changes since v0.9 (identity-capture guard):** launch reservations classify direct SessionStart capture as new, exact resume, or untrusted resume. Only a direct running-harness identity may populate `harness_session_id`; a conflicting capture is quarantined rather than replacing the acting-registry value.

> One sharp knife in a sheath. TAP moves bytes + wakes a pane. It interprets nothing.

**Changes since v0.6 (v0.7 acceptance contract):** `tap send` exit `0` now means durably accepted, with JSON `status=injected|queued`; `delivery=pane|spool|poll` names the disposition. A retryable park and poll delivery are `0/queued`, while verified pane submission is `0/injected`. Human queued receipts use `tap send: queued <id>`. Send exit `4` is retired: denylisted and unsupported harnesses are terminal exit-1 refusals (`unsafe_target` / `unsupported_harness`) with no canonical or spool artifact; exits `3` and `5` retain their meanings. `tap reply` settles for either exit-0 status and validates queued reply evidence from a courier-owned spool payload plus its metadata sidecar (including the spool-to-canonical race). Watch records one exact queued probe id, gives that standard-policy entry a delivery deadline equal to `probe_timeout_seconds`, and starts no response timer until the id reaches canonical/settled state; a still-undeliverable probe raises one sticky NOTIFY at the deadline. The former rc4 defer ladder is retired. Goalie accepts only the exact durable tuples `0:injected:pane`, `0:queued:spool`, `0:queued:poll`, or `5:pending:<empty delivery>` and fails closed on malformed/unknown output. Batching is outside v0.7.

**Protocol version (slot 1 of the sentinel; no `=` since v0.23): 0.23.** The stamp is informational and advances with observable protocol behavior. Historical session-store behavior is superseded by the v0.20 acting-registry contract above; git history retains the retired release details.

The v0.4 cumulative clause above is historical: same-team sentinels **remained** unchanged at that v0.4 wave; the v0.20 registry flip does **not** change same-team sentinel wire shape (tap-cnfm — schema-cut only).
**Changes since v0.12 (absent-seat reclaim):** a pane-backed row becomes reclaimable only after both its stable pane identity and recorded address are observed gone. A matching live seat remains authoritative; an unobservable tmux server never licenses automatic replacement.

**Changes since v0.10 (historical native-id discovery):** store-scanning discovery is retired as runtime authority. Current runtime accepts a native `harness_session_id` only from an explicit acting-registry row or a direct running-harness capture; when the row has no explicit id, the harness chooses its own latest or picker behavior.

**Changes since v0.8 (lifecycle visibility):** current listings and launch collision decisions read the acting registry only. An on-roster row whose pane is absent is shown as `GONE` and routes through the explicit resume/different/fresh picker; an off-roster name starts fresh without consulting departed history.

**Changes since v0.7 (ensure-seat ladder):** named launch uses the acting registry to distinguish an off-roster fresh launch from an on-roster LIVE or GONE collision. Interactive collisions offer resume, different name, or fresh replacement; non-interactive collisions refuse with exit 2.

**Changes since v0.5 (delivery-policy wave):** this release established durable send acceptance, delivery-class receipts, delivery-policy normalization, crew support, `tap team down`, and pane reentry. Its historical session-store lifecycle was superseded by v0.20 registry authority.

**Team reset archives:** `tap team reset` writes a copy-verified bundle with `COMPLETE` last, `tap team restore` restores the latest or an exact bundle, and exact-target `tap team purge` is the deletion path. Older complete bundles are never rotated implicitly.

**Communication identity (tap-ehv + tap-0zv4):** recipient authority is the tuple `(team, handle, pane_id)`, not a bare handle or mutable pane address. A send binds and saves the exact target registry before recipient lookup or any `reply_to` resolution, then requires `registry pane_id == send-time pane_id == live pane_id` at each recipient-effect boundary inside the shared submit helper (interrupt, input clear, stuck-sentinel submit, injection, and Enter). A same-team explicit `--pane` may confirm the registered pane but cannot override it. Bare `reply_to` is resolved against and stamped with the sender's team at send time. This also supersedes tap-rd3's historical “never registry-resolved” readiness wording above: a spool drain does re-read the exact registry bound and stored by the originating send, but never performs fresh team discovery or a bare-handle lookup in the drain caller's registry.

**Harness session identity capture:** `tap register` / `tap in` may read direct native identity from the running harness environment or hook payload and write it to the acting-registry row. A current accepted capture, whether it confirms the authoritative id or adopts a matched replacement, clears `quarantined_harness_session_id`, `quarantined_transcript_path`, and `identity_quarantine_reason` in the same registry commit. A quarantined capture refreshes those annotations; a registration with no current capture decision leaves their history unchanged pending the U4 ruling. For Claude, such retained history remains reachable through the resume chain's disclosed third rung. The acting registry is the only runtime capture store. Capture never scans harness stores, re-stamps another row, or treats `derived_harness_session_id` as resume authority. **A launcher-expectation quarantine is WITHDRAWABLE by the operator** (tap-zqcd): `tap register <h> --accept-observed-identity` retires the standing expectation and accepts the observed id, recording `retired_expectation` and `mode: operator_accepted` on the capture result — the withdrawal is part of the record, not merely applied to it, so an identity a human waved through never reads as an attested one. It is scoped to that class alone; a duplicate-owner refusal is a fact about another seat and no operator statement clears it. A launcher expectation is ALSO retired without an operator, on a seat's own self-attested `clear`, `compact` or `fork` SessionStart, recording `mode: self_attested_rebind` — never `operator_accepted`, because no human was present and the record whose whole job is provenance must not claim a human act. It is admitted only when the seat reports a DIFFERENT id from the one its row is acting on, from its own environment rather than store attribution, and from a process proven by ancestry to run under the pane being registered; `startup` and `resume` are excluded, and a duplicate-owner refusal still clears nothing.

**Context command mode (tap-8cb, v0.5 accumulator):** `tap command compact|clear` adds a strict local-peer context-recovery allowlist, derives its initiator from the live caller pane, targets a stored stable `pane_id` ahead of its mutable address, and writes auto-settled `kind=command` audit payloads. Clear is explicit-seat-only, prepares its immutable audit outcomes before destructive input, publishes `command_status: cleared` at the verified point of no return, and completes only after re-registration plus standing-brief recovery and a linked terminal audit; the judgment gate remains in `/tap:context`.

Codex recovery uses a session id held by the exact bound process, then validates that session’s rollout metadata against the root; a cwd-wide rollout scan cannot select identity. Direct hook/env identity wins and performs no filesystem inference.

**Legacy launcher-store migration:** `registry_migrate.py` is the sole reader of `~/.tap/launcher/sessions.json`; `TAP_SESSION_MANIFEST` is retained only as that migrator's legacy-source override. Normal launch, register, completion, lifecycle, and administration paths neither read nor write the file.
