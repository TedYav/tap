# Stuck-readiness alarm

TAP's courier (the process that delivers queued messages into agent panes) raises this alarm for a seat whose queued messages keep waiting because the seat looks like it is still booting or has no prompt, even though its harness shows its turn ended. It reports once per episode and never acts on the pane; it reuses the `DELIVERY HELD` notice path described in the [per-harness submit + verify table](./per-harness-submit-verify-table-courier-owned-documented-here.md) (tap-bprtg).

A seat whose held mail reads as `busy:booting`, `busy:noprompt` or `busy:overprint` continuously for the bound (`TAP_STUCK_READINESS_SECONDS`, default 600) gets one `DELIVERY STALLED` notice per stuck episode. The alarm fires only while its harness positively shows its last turn ended and no harness activity since the episode began. The drain's Phase A observes each target's first queued payload once per pass, and the alarm reports only: it sends no pane input and changes no delivery, custody, deadline or force decision. The notice goes, through the `DELIVERY HELD` path of the [per-harness submit + verify table](./per-harness-submit-verify-table-courier-owned-documented-here.md), to the sender of the payload observed and to the supervision-watch armer. Each copy is skipped when its addressee is the stuck seat, and the leader copy is skipped when the leader is also the sender. Notice ids are binding-scoped (`stall-<handle>-<binding key>-<episode start>`, plus `-leader`), so a retired payload cannot mint a second copy. A copy that failed to publish retries alone.

| Harness | Turn-ended evidence (all required) | No evidence, so no alarm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude | Registered `transcript_path`, else `<harness_session_id>.jsonl` under the Claude projects dir. In its last 256 KiB, the last user/assistant entry is an assistant with `stop_reason` `end_turn` or `stop_sequence`. Its mtime is the activity clock. | Missing or relative path, unreadable file, a newest record that does not parse, no turn marker in the tail (all unknown); a trailing `tool_use` or user entry (a turn in progress) |
| Codex | Registered `transcript_path` (rollout). The last `task_started`/`task_complete`/`turn_aborted` event is not `task_started`. Its mtime is the activity clock. | No registered path, unreadable file, a newest record that does not parse, no task event in the tail (all unknown); an open task (in progress) |
| OpenCode 2 | The session's newest `session_message` row by native sequence has type `idle`, for any outcome (succeeded, failed or interrupted). Its `time_created` dates the turn end. Creation or completion time is the activity clock. | No session id, unreadable store, or a newer non-idle row, including user/synthetic input or assistant output |
| agy, Cursor, Hermes | No instrument | Always |

Only positive recovery ends an episode and re-arms the alarm: a `ready` or `gone` read, a turn in progress, or harness activity after the episode began (a still-stuck read then starts a new episode). An unknown observation leaves an alarmed episode alone and restarts an unalarmed one. Unknown means a missing instrument, another hold verdict, or a gap in observation longer than the bound. So a continuously stuck seat alarms once. The episode record is `<bus home>/seat-state/<handle>.<binding key>.stall.json`, where the binding key is 16 hex digits of sha256(target registry, handle): a handle alone is not a binding across teams sharing a bus. Its fields are `{schema: 1, seat, registry, pane_id, instance, since, last_seen, notified}`; a different `pane_id` or `instance` is a new episode in place. Its only writer and only reader is the drain's stall hook, which serializes observation, publication and recording under a per-binding kernel lock. Unknown evidence neither alarms nor counts as recovery. Every read or write failure means no alarm; an episode update that does not write cannot alarm. Reset checkpoints it with `seat-state/`, and `tap teardown <handle>` archives it with `<handle>.hash` (matched on the exact 16-hex key, so a dotted neighbour handle is never swept).
