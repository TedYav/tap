## Crew process lifetime (2026-10-28)

Crew lifetime is independent of natural parent exit, crash or relaunch. Stopped
crew rows retain their native conversation, recipe and durable owner link,
including through refresh --hard. Explicit crew removal or parent/team shutdown
still retires the selected family under the existing obligation gates.

New crews start no parent-death helper. Pre-upgrade helpers transition only when
touched: their exact token is fenced and helper generation stopped, with the
binding retained as evidence. Untouched helpers keep their old behavior.
`crew rearm` is retired without effects. See [crew ownership](../identity/crew-ownership.md)
for authority, transition refusals, native adoption and shutdown obligations.
