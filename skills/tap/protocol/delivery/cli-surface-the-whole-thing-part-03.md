## Crew process lifetime (2026-09-25)

New crews are supervised by a finite Bun helper bound to the unique registered
parent's actual harness process generation, seat instance, pane, and tmux server
generation. A harness started manually under a still-live shell is bound by its
foreground process identity. Its exit or relaunch ends its crew, including when
the shell and session remain. A crew can still be dismissed explicitly sooner.

The helper and a pane-local TAP option retain the binding after roster-only
retirement of both rows. It ends only the exact owned child pane after positive
parent-generation loss; an unreadable process or tmux observation is not proof
of exit. A child-only `tap crew relaunch` replaces the child binding while
keeping the original parent generation. A missing helper is reported as
unsupervised by roster and doctor; `tap crew rearm <crew>` binds an existing
live crew only while its unique parent remains provable. Saved native harness
sessions remain manually recoverable after parent exit.
