A slash command sent as part of one large `send-keys -l` burst gets collapsed by Claude Code into a `[Pasted text]` chip; slash-command parsing is disabled inside that pasted blob, so `/compact`, `/goal`, and `/loop` submitted as plain prose instead of real commands — and the ordinary landed-check counted the chip as evidence the command landed, reporting false success. The native courier (`runtime/bun/src/courier.ts`, `submitVerify`) fixes this with an optional **typed-prefix stage**, requested per call through `SubmitAuthority.typedPrefix`. The Bash helper and its `TAP_SV_TYPED_PREFIX` environment variable are retired (R4, 2026-09-24); `tap self` and nothing else passes the field.

- **Absent means the single-stage path.** A call without `typedPrefix` injects the wire once, exactly as before. Because the field belongs to one call's arguments, no value can leak into a later call; the Bash rule about invocation-scoped assignments has no native counterpart.
- **When present, injection becomes two stages.** The ordinary pre-inject gates run unchanged (authorize, copy-mode, band read, draft clobber-with-backup, final authorize), then the effect is admitted before the prefix, because the prefix keystrokes are the first pane effect. Stage 1 types the prefix as its own `send-keys -l` call, settles `0.2`s, and re-authorizes. Stage 2 preserves the payload bytes in UTF-8-safe chunks of at most 250 bytes, planned by `controlPayloadChunks` (tap-v5zii). The courier authorizes the pane identity and its fences before each chunk, sends each chunk once, and settles `0.2`s between chunks. Pacing avoids Claude's paste-collapse heuristic; it is not arrival evidence. Uncertain chunks are never resent.
- **Control focus tail (tap-7r3fn.79).** After stage 2, up to six samples of `controlTailReady` read the chrome-stripped active input region and require its final 128 non-whitespace characters to match the payload suffix, including the complete appended `[[sv:<id>]]` marker. Whitespace folding tolerates physical display wraps; this proves ordered-stream tail arrival, not byte-for-byte integrity of opaque pasted content. The loop waits without retyping; if the tail stays absent, opaque, or mismatched, the result is `unverified` ("control tail absent/opaque; Enter withheld") and no Enter is sent. **On this arm a paste chip is a refusal, never landing evidence.** Ordinary TAP doorbells retain their existing fresh-chip acceptance.
- **The Enter loop is the staged loop.** A landed control goes through the same `submitStagedLocked` loop as every other wire, with the full `prefix+payload` as the expected composer text: four attempts, each reading a fresh frame, with the Cursor-only second Enter. The retired Bash arm ran the legacy five-attempt loop with an unconditional second Enter; that difference is deliberate. A repainting spinner or status band above the composer is not the composer and does not read as a changed composer (tap-eynht; pinned by a control built from a real mid-turn self-compact frame).
- **The trailing-space rule.** The prefix MUST be non-blank text ending in exactly one trailing space (`/compact `, `/loop 5m `), or the call is refused (`unverified`) before touching the pane. That one space is load-bearing: it is what commits Claude Code's slash-command token and closes the autocomplete popup, so the payload injected in stage 2 lands as an argument to an already-parsed command rather than becoming its own (possibly paste-collapsed) top-level input. Zero trailing spaces or two-or-more are both refused — the rule is exactly one, not "at least one."
- **Refused combinations, checked before any pane effect.** A typed prefix is incompatible with queue-prefix delivery (`noPromptOk`, i.e. hermes), with urgent delivery, and with single-Enter callers — none of them ever carries a slash-command payload, so the combination is refused (`unverified`) rather than guessed at.
- **After the first key, every refusal is `unverified` (N1).** Once the prefix may have been typed, a refusal never reports a pre-effect class such as `rate_limited` or `pane_gone`: a caller that retried on those would type onto a partial composer. The same holds for a thrown failure: a tmux server that stops answering after the first prefix key may have landed returns `unverified` with nothing replayed, never a generic error.
- **Claude feedback panel:** the native reader recognizes a closed “Bug report drafted” panel with the exact review/send/dismiss controls immediately above the composer. Pre-inject readiness and urgent's first key consult that reader. After injection, ordinary Enter and nudge withhold under the panel even with attested liveness, and queue-prefix authority does not override it (tap-89a57 reversed the earlier liveness override). Native readiness names it `busy:feedback-draft` (tap-89a57; formerly the generic `busy:modal`) and the courier parks under `feedback-draft`, which force, no-spool and no-prompt paths cannot bypass. See [Claude feedback-draft hold](per-harness-submit-verify-table-courier-owned-documented-here.md#claude-feedback-draft-hold-tap-89a57-2026-10-01). Box extraction shares native geometry while preserving draft bytes for Bash and normalizing only comparison text. Recovery is manual: dismiss the dialog, inspect and preserve input, then use `tap poll` or `tap read` for the canonical message. A truncated marker never authorizes automatic Escape, clearing, reconstruction or reinjection.
- **Codex composer reader:** native readiness and Enter verification, Bash composer reads, staged-sentinel recovery, and nudge share the native reader. After the existing modal checks, an exact dim-painted `Ask Codex to do anything` composer row proves empty input regardless of footer rows beneath it; unstyled placeholder words remain typed input. For other composer rows, it removes the trailing model/known-effort footer with either `Context N% left` or `directory · weekly N% left`, including terminal `left`, `left…` and `left Goal paused (/goal resume)` endings and up to four physical rows needed to complete the footer skeleton. Rows after an already recognized footer remain ambiguous input; this conservative extraction remains the pre-inject draft policy. Ordinary Enter and nudge instead use visible complete-text containment; unknown effort/footer rows and extra input do not veto it. Missing or truncated expected text still withholds Enter. Nudge retains its recipient and pane-identity checks.
- **Claude hard-row control rendering:** exact whole-composer comparison remains first. For an emitted one-line ASCII `/compact`, `/goal`, or `/loop` control ending in its `[[sv:...]]` marker, the shared frame matcher may compare Claude continuation rows against the complete expected string. It removes only the demonstrated two-column continuation indentation and display padding for comparison. Each row break must coincide with one expected single space. Both observed renderer shapes are supported: legacy rows reserve four columns; newer rows may fill box width minus three. Claude 2.1.280 does not wrap at a fixed width: a word ending at box width minus four sometimes wraps, so a break is allowed when the next word would reach either of the last two content columns (tap-arm4w: 38 live frames at 69, 100 and 145 columns). Short splits, blank rows, changed text or within-row spacing, incomplete matches, multiline expected text, and unknown character widths remain refusals. Draft/payload bytes are never rewritten. A typed newline placed exactly at a natural visual wrap is observationally indistinguishable; this is a rendering-equality limit, not a claim to detect every whitespace edit. The wire-sentinel whitespace normalizer remains restricted to wire sentinels.
- **Withheld own injection (tap-arm4w).** When the pre-Enter guard withholds a submit's own freshly typed input and no Enter was sent, the submitter takes it back: the `clear-own` door clears the composer only if its text equals the injected wire up to whitespace (wraps and indentation). On the courier's typed arm every take-back key first passes the same copy-mode, fresh owner (registry row) and reset/clear-fence checks as Enter; a refusal sends no key and reports `take-back: refused (…)`. "No Enter was sent" counts only an Enter the tmux client positively refused (rate-limited); an Enter that failed any other way may have landed, so no take-back follows it. After a verified typed submit, the optional short-draft paste-back reads the pane inside one error boundary: a failure there keeps the verified result and the retained backup. Merged, altered, or unreadable input stays untouched and is reported. The result stays `5`; nothing was submitted, and nothing is retried. Without it, a withhold leaves the seat's composer occupied and deaf to delivery.

- **Codex hook review (tap-swky4):** the live “Hooks need review” menu with its review/trust/continue choices and terminal `enter confirm · esc skip` footer is `codex-hook-trust`, a blocked human decision even under a stale working title. A later composer makes the old menu historical. Launcher readiness checks fresh and resumed Codex seats, reports the frame and retains a blocked launch for inspection; it does not claim ready. Courier delivery parks under the named reason with the operator follow-up, never as occupied composer, and sends no keys even with urgent, force or no-spool. No automatic trust or install-time pre-trust is permitted. The lead investigates and may safely skip or decline the dialog, then tells the operator: hooks were not trusted; TAP's shell-body guard and SessionStart hooks will not run in this seat until trusted via Codex's hook review. Dismissal does not grant trust; the courier never dismisses it automatically.

  Restart preserves the named dialog result and attach action. Retained panes restore their original `remain-on-exit` setting. Bulk resume reports retained dialogs as `manual_attention` (exit 2 when no ordinary failures remain), excludes them from fresh-start recovery, and counts them separately from resumed or failed seats. In-place relaunch carries the observed blocker frame into its exit-7 diagnosis without a second capture that could relabel or lose it.
