On the human `tap attach` door, omitting a target opens team then live-seat
selection. The team step offers only teams holding at least one live attachable
seat; daemon and pane-less infrastructure rows never count, and a machine with
no live attachable seat refuses (`no_candidates`) instead of opening an empty
picker. A valid seat miss in a known team stays scoped to that team and seeds
the seat query; an unknown qualified team seeds team selection before the seat
query. Exact rostered seats still use the ordinary attach classifier and stopped
seat resume offer. Malformed input and corrupt, old, missing-index, or unreachable
authority refuse before selection. JSON, redirected input/output, and unusable
terminals preserve explicit-target behavior. A picked seat is attach-only: the
selected enrollment, row generation, and observed pane must still match at the
client move, or the command refuses without a second fuzzy lookup.
